Reconstructing complex light
with phase-only holograms.

Shuyang Xie · Wuhan University · MATLAB / Fourier optics / numerical validation

The problem

A vortex field is defined by both its ring-shaped amplitude and its winding phase. A phase-only modulator cannot directly prescribe both. My research examines whether an established bidirectional error diffusion method (BERD) can encode a custom annular complex field while preserving its amplitude, phase structure, and topological charge.

What I built

  • A parameterized target model: a Gaussian ring amplitude multiplied by exp(iℓθ), with independently specified radius, width, and topological charge.
  • An end-to-end MATLAB workflow: zero-padding, angular-spectrum back-propagation, BERD phase-only encoding, and Fresnel reconstruction, adapted from existing propagation and encoding modules.
  • A consistent evaluation framework: reference fields rebuilt on the output sampling grid; global phase and scale alignment; amplitude, wrapped-phase, complex-field, energy-distribution, and topological-charge metrics.
  • A controlled six-case study: fixed ring geometry and propagation parameters, varying only ℓ across −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, and 4.

Representative result · ℓ = 1

Original simulation images, center displayed at 3× zoom. This is a display crop; metrics use the original evaluation region.

At ℓ = 1, amplitude correlation is 0.990634, complex-field correlation is 0.919015, and phase RMSE is 0.266096 rad. The recovered topological charge is 1.

The study uses λ = 671 nm, a 70 mm propagation distance, a ring radius of 260 μm, and a width parameter of 35 μm. The 2500×2500 target is embedded in a 5000×5000 padded grid; metrics are evaluated within a 501×501 output window and the specified target support.

What the results reveal

From ℓ = 1 to ℓ = 4, amplitude correlation stays nearly unchanged, while complex-field correlation falls from 0.919015 to 0.909896. All tested topological orders remain correct. This distinguishes global phase winding from local field fidelity: a correct vortex order does not imply error-free reconstruction.

Engineering judgment

The most consequential implementation details were physical sampling consistency, removing irrelevant global phase offsets, fitting overall amplitude and complex scales, and inspecting mirrored diffusion weights for reverse scanning. These checks make the reported metrics more interpretable than a visual comparison of ring images alone.

VALIDATION SCOPE

Numerical simulation using an established algorithm. Physical SLM validation and systematic algorithm baselines were not completed. The 8-bit hologram is an export artifact; reported reconstruction metrics use the unquantized phase array. Correlation is not percentage accuracy, and target-window energy concentration is not device diffraction efficiency.
