04 / INSIDE THE RESEARCHZTD → PWV

The signal is delayed.
The atmosphere leaves a clue.

PWV is the depth of liquid water that would remain if all water vapor in an atmospheric column condensed. It is useful for weather and climate monitoring.

GNSS signals carry a total delay, but only part of that delay comes from water vapor. Conventional retrieval uses pressure and weighted mean temperature to separate and convert the components.

The research question: can ZTD, location, elevation and time provide enough information to infer PWV directly?

PHYSICAL RELATIONSHIP
ZTD = ZHD + ZWD
PWV = Π(Tm) × ZWD

Hydrostatic + wet delay · conversion depends on atmospheric temperature

DIRECT ML RETRIEVAL

Position and time carry statistical information about the atmospheric background. The task estimates PWV at observation time.

Study context & data provenance

The original study is documented in an unpublished research manuscript. Its reported figures are labeled “Manuscript study.” The workbench uses subsequent archived CSV, SHAP and benchmark outputs, with model and sampling context shown beside each view.

Map summaries weight station errors by observation count. The map preserves low-count and high-error groups; an individual point may be less stable than the global summary. No raw observations, model weights or complete manuscript are distributed here.
