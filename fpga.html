01 / WHAT THE SYSTEM DOESCAMERA → PIXELS → TARGET INFORMATION

Process the image
as the pixels arrive.

A team competition project: build a demonstrable color-and-shape recognition pipeline for a specified FPGA platform.

The project integrates camera, video-buffering, and display IP with custom vision RTL. Pixel-stream processing produces segmentation masks, frame-level region features, and geometric decisions.

MY CONTRIBUTION

We worked collaboratively across the system. My main contributions were in the camera pipeline, two-pixel processing, and computer vision modules, particularly color segmentation.

01

Pipeline the arithmetic.

Integer coefficients, shifts, and lookup tables implement color-space calculations. Shape predicates use cross multiplication instead of directly dividing ratios.

02

Keep two pixels and their control signals aligned.

The data path processes two pixels per clock. Region accumulators advance coordinates with the stream and latch area and bounding boxes at frame boundaries.

03

Classify with interpretable geometry.

Valid-area thresholds, bounding-box fill, and aspect ratio produce three shape codes. The rules are compact and debuggable, while still depending on viewpoint and object pose.

04

Inspect pixels and system outputs together.

Tune thresholds with mask previews, inspect intermediate signals with a logic analyzer, then compare the displayed box and LED-coded output.
